# Map data attribution

The compact Nairobi CBD road geometry is based on OpenStreetMap (OSM) centerline data.

- Copyright: © OpenStreetMap contributors
- License: Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL) v1.0
- Source: https://www.openstreetmap.org/
- Export: `Assets/Data/nairobi-cbd-roads.geojson`

This project includes a small, simplified road subset for gameplay. It is not a complete or navigation-grade map. Redistributors must retain this attribution and provide ODbL notice. If you modify or expand the data, share adapted database data under the ODbL when applicable.

The GeoJSON source data and Unity world conversion are separate: geographic coordinates are projected locally around 36.8188°E, 1.2867°S for a playable scene.
